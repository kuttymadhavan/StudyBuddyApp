Welcome to Gurukul

Your own study companion, on your device

Gurukul turns your textbook into notes, questions, flashcards, practice tests and a step-by-step maths workbench. It keeps working when the internet does not, and your work stays private to you.

📚 Your book, made yours

Upload a chapter and get notes, explanations, Q&A, flashcards and a test written around it.

🧮 Practice that explains

The maths workbench shows one step at a time, with hints, a second method and a question for you to try.

🌱 Progress you can see

A study galaxy, a daily plan, an error book and reports help you see what is improving and what needs another look.

🔒 Private by design

Your books, notes and progress stay on this device. AI is optional. Nothing is sold or shown to advertisers.

How joining works

Ask for access with your details. Your organiser checks the request and emails you an invite code. Enter that code once to activate Gurukul, then create your local account and start studying.
